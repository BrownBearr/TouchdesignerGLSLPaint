Brush textures (PNG)
====================

Put your custom brush textures in this folder (or anywhere on disk) and upload them in the app UI.

Format requirements:
- PNG with transparency (RGBA recommended)
- White pixels = paint coverage
- Black pixels = transparent (no paint)
- Alpha channel is respected (coverage is multiplied by alpha)

Tips:
- A soft-edged circular brush works well as a starter.
- High-contrast bristle textures give a more “oil paint” feel.
- Keep the brush image reasonably sized (e.g. 256x256). The app rescales it per brush radius.
